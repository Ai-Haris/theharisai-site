What is new in OMNITUBE 2, and do I have to pay again?

What is Omni Shorts?

Omni Shorts (Pro) turns a topic into a finished Short: script, voice, word-synced captions, animated 3D or 2D shots, sound and music, rendered to MP4 on your PC. Pick one of 8 styles, a voice and a length. It also makes long videos in chapters, many Shorts at once (Batch Builder) and an upload kit with titles, tags and a thumbnail.

Does Omni Shorts need an API key? What does a Short cost?

No key is needed: a free AI model can write the video on your PC. For the best look, add an OpenRouter key and pick Claude Sonnet as the writer, about $0.08 per Short. Voice, captions and rendering are always free on your PC. You can also make it with Claude Desktop.

Is OMNITUBE 2 on Mac?

Yes. OMNITUBE 2 runs on Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and later, macOS 12 or newer). Mac users on 1.5 get it from the update button in the app, or download the new Mac version above. Omni Shorts on Mac encodes with the Mac's own video engine.

How much does a cloud video cost, and how do I keep it low?

Cloud mode (Pro) uses your own AI accounts, so you pay the providers directly and OMNITUBE adds nothing. Before every cloud build you see a cost range, and after it the real amount spent. To keep it low: set Pacing to Calm on the Storyboard, make a short test video first, use the free voices, and make pictures on your PC if you have an NVIDIA RTX card.

My graphics card is older (GTX 10 series, Quadro P). Can I use OMNITUBE?

Yes. The picture and video engine needs an RTX 20 series card or newer, so on older cards OMNITUBE 1.5 sets up the light version: voices, scripts, captions, music and editing run on your PC, and OMNITUBE Pro makes the pictures and clips in the cloud on your own key. You skip the big local download.

I bought Pro. How do I move it to a new PC?

Open the Pro page on the new PC and activate your key, or use Move Pro to another PC. The first move works right away, after that once every 30 days. Reinstalling on the same PC never needs a move, just activate the same key again.

Where is the full guide?

Inside the app: click How to use in the sidebar. It explains every screen and option, the script format with examples, costs, troubleshooting and where to get each account key. The same guide is also a PDF: download the OMNITUBE User Guide (PDF, 45 pages).

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

That message appears for new apps that are not yet widely downloaded. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". OMNITUBE installs only into the folder you choose.

I'm on a Mac. How do I open it?

OMNITUBE for Mac is for Apple Silicon (M1 and later). Drag OMNITUBE to your Applications folder, then open it. The first time, macOS may say it could not verify the app. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway, then open OMNITUBE again. This is only needed once.

Faster fix using Terminal: Open Terminal and paste this command, then press Return. After that, open OMNITUBE normally.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/OMNITUBE.app

If OMNITUBE still does not open and Terminal says it was built for a newer macOS, download the Mac version again from this page. Versions 1.4.5 and newer run on macOS 12 Monterey and newer.

A signed version is on the way.

"Smart App Control blocked an app" or "Error 4551". What do I do?

Smart App Control is a Windows 11 setting that blocks every new app that is not yet code-signed, including OMNITUBE. A signed version is on the way. Until then:

  1. Open Windows Security (search it in the Start menu).
  2. Go to App & browser control, then Smart App Control settings.
  3. Choose Off, then run the OMNITUBE installer again.

On Windows 11 with the April 2026 update or newer you can switch Smart App Control back on later from the same page. On older versions, turning it off is permanent until Windows is reinstalled, so only do it if you are comfortable with that.

I already have OMNITUBE. How do I get the new version?

Open OMNITUBE and click the update button in the top bar. It downloads the new version and restarts in a few seconds. Your projects, voices and models stay as they are. You can also download the new installer here and install it into the same folder. See What's new for every version.

Do I own the videos?

Yes. Your scripts and videos never leave your PC. The included models use open licenses (Apache 2.0 and MIT). Follow YouTube's rules: add your own creativity and mark realistic AI content as altered or synthetic when YouTube asks.

I have an AMD or Intel graphics card.

OMNITUBE runs in Limited mode: voices, scripts, captions, music, sound effects, stock footage and editing all work. AI images and video clips on your own PC need an NVIDIA RTX card. OMNITUBE Pro makes them with cloud AI on any PC.

My antivirus flags OMNITUBE (for example Avast "IDP.Generic").

That is a false positive: the app is new and not code-signed yet, and some antivirus tools flag every new unsigned program. Add the OMNITUBE folder as an exception, or restore the file from the antivirus quarantine. A signed version is on the way.

Omni Shorts setup stops with "No module named pip".

The next update (2.0.3) fixes this by itself. Until then, open Command Prompt, paste this line and press Enter:

"%USERPROFILE%\.omni-shorts\venv\Scripts\python.exe" -m ensurepip --upgrade

Then open Setup Center, Omni Shorts, and click Set up now again. Still stuck? Send us the file setup.log from the .omni-shorts folder in your user folder.

Something is not working. How do I get help?

Open Setup Center and click Repair OMNITUBE, it fixes most problems. Still stuck? Click Create error report in the same place and send the file from your Desktop to @theharisai.
