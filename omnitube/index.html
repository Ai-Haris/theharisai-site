How to use

Write a script, get a video

OMNITUBE turns a plain text script into a finished video. Pick your mode, write your script and press Make my video.

Script format

Every script follows the same pattern. Use it in any mode.

# Declare characters once at the top
[CAST: Zara = 28, red jacket, short dark hair, confident]
[CAST: Leo = 35, grey suit, glasses, calm]

# Each heading is a new scene

# The Discovery

# Describe the visual for each scene
[IMG: rooftop at sunset, Zara leaning on railing, city below, golden light, wide shot]

# Plain text becomes the narrator voice
She had been here a thousand times. But tonight felt different.

# The Conversation

[IMG: rooftop, Zara and Leo facing each other, tense, close shot]

Leo stepped out of the shadows. "You found it, didn't you."
Mode 1

Single Story

A complete standalone video from one script. Every heading is a scene, with its own image prompt and narration. Great for fairy tales, explainers, mini-documentaries and short films.

  • Declare [CAST:] once at the top
  • Use # headings for each scene
  • [IMG:] sets the visual for that scene
  • Plain text below becomes narration
  • Everything in one file, one video
Mode 2

Season

A multi-episode series where characters and the world carry over between episodes. Reuse the same [CAST:] names and OMNITUBE keeps faces consistent across all of them.

  • Same cast names across all episodes
  • Shared world, growing story arc
  • Each episode is its own script file
  • OMNITUBE links them into a series
  • Great for drama, sci-fi, story series
Mode 3

Rhyme

Rhythmic, poem-style scripts where every line sings. The AI matches visuals to the beat of the words. Perfect for kids content, nursery rhymes and short poetic social clips.

  • Write in rhyming couplets or verses
  • [IMG:] sets the scene for each verse
  • Pacing follows the rhythm of the text
  • No [CAST:] needed, imagery-driven
  • Short, punchy, made for sharing

Not sure where to start? Open OMNITUBE, pick Single Story, choose a channel style, paste any short story and press Make my video.

FAQ

Questions

Is OMNITUBE really free?

Yes. There is no watermark, no subscription and no limit on minutes or videos. It runs on your own computer, so making more videos costs nothing extra.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

That message appears for new apps that are not yet widely downloaded. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". OMNITUBE installs only into the folder you choose.

I'm on a Mac. How do I open it?

OMNITUBE for Mac is for Apple Silicon (M1 and later). Drag OMNITUBE to your Applications folder, then open it. The first time, macOS may say it could not verify the app. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway, then open OMNITUBE again. This is only needed once.

Faster fix using Terminal: Open Terminal and paste this command, then press Return. After that, open OMNITUBE normally.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/OMNITUBE.app

A signed version is on the way.

"Smart App Control blocked an app" or "Error 4551". What do I do?

Smart App Control is a Windows 11 setting that blocks every new app that is not yet code-signed, including OMNITUBE. A signed version is on the way. Until then:

  1. Open Windows Security (search it in the Start menu).
  2. Go to App & browser control, then Smart App Control settings.
  3. Choose Off, then run the OMNITUBE installer again.

On Windows 11 with the April 2026 update or newer you can switch Smart App Control back on later from the same page. On older versions, turning it off is permanent until Windows is reinstalled, so only do it if you are comfortable with that.

I already have OMNITUBE. How do I get the new version?

Open OMNITUBE and click the update button in the top bar. It downloads the new version and restarts in a few seconds. Your projects, voices and models stay as they are. You can also download the new installer here and install it into the same folder. See What's new for every version.

Does it need the internet?

Only for the first setup (downloading the AI models) and for updates. After that, videos are made fully offline.

Do I own the videos?

Yes. Your scripts and videos never leave your PC. The included models use open licenses (Apache 2.0 and MIT). Follow YouTube's rules: add your own creativity and mark realistic AI content as altered or synthetic when YouTube asks.

I have an AMD or Intel graphics card.

OMNITUBE runs in Limited mode: voices, scripts, captions, music, sound effects, stock footage and editing all work. AI images and video clips on your own PC need an NVIDIA RTX card. OMNITUBE Pro makes them with cloud AI on any PC.

My antivirus flags OMNITUBE (for example Avast "IDP.Generic").

That is a false positive: the app is new and not code-signed yet, and some antivirus tools flag every new unsigned program. Add the OMNITUBE folder as an exception, or restore the file from the antivirus quarantine. A signed version is on the way.

A build stopped with an error. Do I start again?

No. Click Resume: finished voices, images and clips are kept and OMNITUBE continues from where it stopped.

Something is not working. How do I get help?

Open Setup Center and click Repair OMNITUBE, it fixes most problems. Still stuck? Click Create error report in the same place and send the file from your Desktop to @theharisai.

What is OMNITUBE Pro? Is it paid?

Pro is an optional paid upgrade, in private testing now. It is the same app with extra power: the Documentary Studio with 21 channel styles, Premiere Pro and CapCut export, cloud AI video and images with your own API keys, ElevenLabs voices, lip sync, multi-GPU and auto publishing. The price will be announced on @theharisai. The free version stays free, with no watermark and no limits.
