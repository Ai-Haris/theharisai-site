How to use

From style to finished video

  1. Pick a style. Open any card and watch the sample. Use open the interactive player to try the facecam and screenshot slots with your own files.
  2. Set your options. Choose voiceover, facecam, Brand UI and format, then paste your topic or full script.
  3. Copy the prompt. Open a new chat at claude.ai and paste it. If you picked Brand UI, attach 1 to 3 screenshots in the same message.
  4. Approve the storyboard. Claude sketches every beat as still frames first. Reply "OK" or ask for changes.
  5. Get your video. Claude builds the animation as a page you can play. Load your voiceover or facecam in its player, or ask Claude to "render the MP4" for a 1080p 60fps file.

With a voiceover

Export a word-level SRT from CapCut, ElevenLabs or Whisper and paste it in the Voiceover timing box. Every on-screen word then lands on its spoken word.

With a facecam

Record yourself first. Choose "Facecam in a corner" or "Full-frame intro, then corner", and the layout keeps text clear of your camera.

Best results

Keep a video to one idea, 15 to 40 seconds, with short punchy lines. Use a Claude plan that can run code if you want the MP4 rendered for you.
