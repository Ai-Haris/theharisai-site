How it works

Five steps, about two minutes. Everything happens in a normal Claude chat.

00:0000:2000:4001:0001:30
STEP 01

Pick a style

Watch it play on the wall above. Open the live player to try the facecam and screenshot slots with your own files.

STEP 02

Set your options

Voiceover, facecam, your screenshots and format. Then paste your topic or full script.

STEP 03

Copy the prompt

Open a new chat at claude.ai and paste it. Picked Brand UI? Attach 1 to 3 screenshots in the same message.

STEP 04

Approve the storyboard

Claude sketches every beat as still frames first. Reply "OK" or ask for changes.

STEP 05

Get your video

Claude builds the animation as a page you can play. Load your voiceover or facecam in its player, or ask it to "render the MP4" for a 1080p 60fps file.

With a voiceover

Export a word-level SRT from CapCut, ElevenLabs or Whisper and paste it in the timing box. Every on-screen word lands on its spoken word.

With a facecam

Record yourself first. Choose "corner" or "full-frame intro, then corner" and the layout keeps text clear of your camera.

Best results

One idea per video, 15 to 40 seconds, short punchy lines. Use a Claude plan that can run code if you want the MP4 rendered for you.
