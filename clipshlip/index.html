New in 1.4 — what 1.3 could not do

Your captions were running late, and you could not have known

Caption images are laid along the clip one after another, and the app measured each one's length wrongly whenever two overlapped. Every overlap was counted twice, so every caption after it landed late — and the error added up as the clip went on.

Measured across 141 caption tracks made by ordinary runs: 35 drift by more than one frame, and the worst ends up 9.9 seconds behind the audio. Its last ten seconds of captions belong to a different part of the clip entirely.

Fixed — all 141 now land on time. But clips you already exported with 1.3 have it baked in. If something you posted felt out of step, this is why. Re-exporting in 1.4 fixes it.

more than a frame late 35/141 → 0/141 worst drift 9.9s → 0.0s

Change where a clip starts and ends

Drag the two handles under the timeline. The clip is re-cut, re-captioned, and your end card, watermark and music go back on — about twenty seconds, because none of the slow analysis runs again. Drag outwards to rescue a word that got cut off.

1.3 — the boundaries were final

Take a word out of the middle

Tick words in the transcript and remove them. The audio goes with them and the clip closes up — for a stumble, a repeated sentence, or a name you would rather not broadcast.

1.3 — nothing could be removed

Fix a misheard name once

Whisper gets a name wrong the same way every time. Correct a word now changes it everywhere in the project at once, and tells you how many it found before it changes anything.

1.3 — once per clip, every clip

Nudge the captions

Move every caption up to half a second either way, watching the preview redraw against the speech. There for the projects where the words land a fraction early or a fraction late.

1.3 — nothing you could do

Turn captions off

Before the run, beside the style: burn them in, or none at all. With none, nothing is drawn on the picture — and your end card, watermark and music are still added, because those are not captions.

1.3 — every clip got captions

Export a clean copy

A Captions: OFF button beside Export hands you the clip with nothing drawn on it. It loses the end card and watermark too — all three are burnt in by the same pass, and the screen says so.

1.3 — only the captioned one

Choose the cover frame

Scrub to any frame and use it as the poster, or save it out as a PNG for a thumbnail.

1.3 — always 20% into the clip

Short clips are captioned, not re-cut

“Upload short clips” said your edit would be left alone and then cut it anyway — an 18.8s clip came back as 17.4s, trimmed at both ends. Now your cut is your cut.

1.3 — it said so and did not do it

Your post copy leaves with the clip

Every export writes a .txt beside the video: title, a caption per platform, hashtags, description and call to action — written so Urdu and Hindi open correctly in Notepad.

1.3 — trapped in the app, one field at a time

It says there is no speech model before the run

Transcription is the third stage, so starting without a model used to mean reading your video and extracting all of its audio first — minutes on a long podcast — and only then admitting it could not transcribe.

1.3 — you found out after the wait

The editor fits on the screen

The style list was a thousand pixels tall, so every control under it sat below the fold, and the middle column had no scrollbar at all — the trim handles and the Export button simply ran off the bottom of the window.

1.3 — Export was unreachable

Roman Urdu reads better

Common words that came out as majhe, mire, chka and bn now come out as mujhe, mere, chuka and ban.

What happens when you press Generate

It listens

02

It picks

03

It reframes

Follows whoever is speaking and crops to vertical, without the seasick camera.
